# thoughtBench Philosophy

## Why this exists
I needed a professional-grade, offline LLM workbench that:
- doesn’t require babysitting a terminal
- respects local-first privacy
- handles real work artifacts (PDF/DOCX/XLSX/images)
- feels like an Apple-native pro app, not a wrapper

If the tool doesn’t match the way builders actually build, it fails.

## Principles
1. **Local-first by default**
   - Conversations, files, and models remain on-device unless explicitly shared.

2. **Storage is user-controlled**
   - Default to Application Support.
   - Allow external drives and custom roots without breaking the app.

3. **Iteration is the core UX**
   - Edit prompt on a specific message.
   - Try again with the same context.
   - Compare outputs.
   - Grade responses to improve your own workflows (and future automation).

4. **File-heavy workflows are first-class**
   - Import, preview, reference, summarize.
   - No “just paste the content” nonsense.

5. **Professional UI**
   - A calm, dribbble-grade interface with strong information hierarchy.
   - Fast navigation. Zero clutter.

6. **No lock-in**
   - Bring your own models (local paths, Hugging Face, internal models).
   - The app adapts to the user, not the other way around.

## What “good” looks like
- A builder can install it and be productive in minutes.
- Everything works offline.
- The app never “forgets where your stuff is.”
